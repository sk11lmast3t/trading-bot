- Symbols are ranked by **current** volume, so the set is biased towards coins that are big today (survivorship). Treat results as optimistic for alts.
- **Risk-limit halts are off** for measurement (drawdown, daily loss, loss-streak, circuit breaker); sizing is the bot's own (1% risk, position capped at 10% of capital), so dollar pnl is small. Compare strategies in **bps of notional** and **R** (R = pnl / that trade's own stop distance).
- Trades cut off by a month boundary are excluded (640 of them).

## 7. What this report does NOT prove
- **Backtester vs live.** The backtester exits on stop, target or the 30 min time limit only. The live bot also arms a breakeven stop and a trailing stop (`risk_manager.py`), which are NOT simulated here. They can change results either way.
- **Intrabar order.** If a bar touches both stop and target, the stop is assumed first (pessimistic). Gaps through a stop fill at the stop price (optimistic).
- **Maker fills** use a simple queue-penetration rule. Real fill rates and adverse selection must be measured live.
- **Fees and slippage** are assumptions (flags above), identical for every symbol. Small alts slip more than BTC.
- **Per-symbol independence.** Each symbol is simulated alone on a fresh $1,000; portfolio caps, margin and exchange limits are not modelled.
- **One market history.** Months here are the months that happened. A regime not present in the data is not tested.
- **Survivorship** in the symbol list (ranked by today's volume).
